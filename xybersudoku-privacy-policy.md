# Privacy Policy for XyberSudoku

**Effective Date:** 16 October 2026  
**Last Updated:** 16 October 2026

XyberSudoku is developed and published by **XYBERNA**.

This Privacy Policy explains how XyberSudoku ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

## 1. About XyberSudoku

XyberSudoku is a Sudoku puzzle game designed to provide an enjoyable, accessible, and privacy-conscious puzzle-solving experience.

The App is intended to support offline gameplay wherever possible. You do not need to create an account to play Sudoku.

## 2. Information We Collect

XyberSudoku is designed to minimise the collection of personal information.

### Information You Provide

The App does not require you to provide your name, email address, telephone number, or other personal details to play the game.

If optional features requiring personal information are introduced in a future version, the relevant information and its purpose will be disclosed appropriately.

### Gameplay Data

The App may store gameplay information locally on your device, including:

- Game progress and unfinished puzzles.
- Difficulty preferences and game settings.
- Completion times, scores, and statistics.
- Hints used and other gameplay-related progress.

This information is used to maintain your progress and improve your gaming experience. Locally stored information generally remains on your device unless you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

### Automatically Collected Information

If advertising, crash reporting, analytics, or other third-party services are integrated into the App, those services may collect technical information, such as:

- Device model and operating system information.
- App interactions and diagnostic information.
- Advertising identifiers, where permitted.
- IP address and information used to estimate general location.
- Advertising performance and fraud-prevention signals.

The information collected depends on which services are enabled and how they are configured.

## 3. Advertising

XyberSudoku may display advertisements through third-party advertising providers, such as Google AdMob, if advertising is enabled in the published version.

Third-party advertising providers may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and related information to deliver advertisements, measure performance, prevent fraud, and support other permitted advertising purposes.

Where required, appropriate consent and privacy controls will be provided. Advertising personalisation and data processing are subject to applicable laws, platform policies, and advertising provider settings.

For information about Google's data practices, visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

If advertising is not enabled in a particular release, this section applies only if advertising features are introduced and activated.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services to provide advertising, crash diagnostics, analytics, or other functionality.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and may process information according to their respective terms and configurations.

XYBERNA does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers. Users should review the applicable policies for further information.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Gameplay progress and preferences may be stored locally on your device.

We take reasonable measures, appropriate to the App's design and available capabilities, to minimise unnecessary data collection and protect information processed through services under our control.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 6. Data Sharing

XYBERNA does not sell your personal information as a direct business practice.

We do not intentionally collect personal information merely to allow you to play the core Sudoku game.

Information may nevertheless be processed by integrated third-party providers, such as advertising or diagnostic services, as necessary for their functions. These providers may process information under their own policies and applicable legal requirements.

We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of the App.

## 7. Children's Privacy

XyberSudoku is a general-purpose puzzle game. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children through the core gameplay experience.

Third-party services, including advertising providers, may have their own age-related requirements and data practices. Where applicable, the App's configuration and advertising practices will be adjusted to comply with relevant children's privacy laws and Google Play policies.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information through a feature controlled by XYBERNA may contact us using the details below.

## 8. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your device, region, and applicable law, you may be able to:

- Reset your device's advertising identifier or manage advertising privacy settings.
- Manage consent for applicable advertising and tracking activities.
- Clear locally stored gameplay data through your device settings.
- Uninstall the App to stop its future operation on your device.
- Exercise applicable legal rights concerning personal information processed by XYBERNA.

Uninstalling the App may remove locally stored progress. Information already processed by third-party providers may be subject to their retention policies and applicable legal requirements.

## 9. Data Retention

Gameplay information stored locally is generally retained until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.

Information processed by third-party services is retained according to the relevant provider's policies, technical configuration, and legal obligations.

Where XYBERNA controls personal information, it will retain that information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose or as required by law.

## 10. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers may process information on servers located in countries other than your own. Where applicable, such processing is subject to the providers' policies and relevant legal safeguards.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, third-party integrations, or legal requirements change.

The revised version will be published at the same privacy policy URL, with an updated **Last Updated** date. Significant changes may also be communicated through appropriate channels where required.
