# XyberSnake Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 16, 2026**

XyberSnake is a mobile game developed and published by **XYBERNA**.

This Privacy Policy explains how XyberSnake handles information when you use the game.

## 1. Information We Collect

XyberSnake does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to play the game.

The game may store gameplay-related information locally on your device, such as:

* Game progress
* Unlocked levels and worlds
* Scores and statistics
* Game settings
* Unlocked cosmetics or other in-game progression

This information is used to provide and maintain the game's functionality.

## 2. Advertising

XyberSnake uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements, including interstitial and rewarded advertisements.

AdMob may collect and process information such as:

* Advertising identifiers
* Device information
* IP address
* Approximate location derived from IP address
* Information about advertisements shown or interacted with
* App and diagnostic information

This information may be used for advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and other purposes described by Google.

For more information about how Google handles information in advertising services, please refer to Google's privacy information and advertising policies.

## 3. Rewarded Advertisements

XyberSnake may provide optional rewarded advertisements.

For example, you may voluntarily choose to watch an advertisement to receive an in-game reward.

Rewarded advertisements are optional. You are not required to watch an advertisement to use the game.

## 4. Third-Party Services

XyberSnake uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Currently, this includes:

**Google AdMob**

AdMob is provided by Google LLC and is used to serve and measure advertisements.

Google's privacy policy is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 5. Data Sharing

XYBERNA does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared by third-party service providers when necessary to provide services used by the game, including advertising and related measurement, security, and fraud-prevention services.

## 6. Children's Privacy

XyberSnake is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

The game does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information.

Where advertising is provided, advertising behavior and configuration are subject to the applicable Google AdMob policies and settings.

## 7. Data Security

XYBERNA takes reasonable measures to protect information associated with the game.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Data Retention

Gameplay information stored locally by XyberSnake generally remains on the user's device until the user deletes the data, uninstalls the game, or the application otherwise removes or resets the stored information.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob is retained according to the applicable third-party service's policies.

## 9. Your Choices

You can:

* Choose whether to watch optional rewarded advertisements.
* Manage advertising-related settings through your Android device and Google account where available.
* Uninstall XyberSnake to remove locally stored game data.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to XyberSnake, its features, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the **Last updated** date at the top of this policy will be updated.

## 11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or XyberSnake's privacy practices, you can contact XYBERNA through the contact information provided on the official XYBERNA website or the Google Play Store listing for XyberSnake.
