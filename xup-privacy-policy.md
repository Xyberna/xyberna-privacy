## 1. Introduction

Welcome to XUP, a communication platform developed and operated by XYBERNA.

At XYBERNA, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how XUP collects, processes, stores, uses and protects information when you access or use our applications and services.

By using XUP, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue using XUP.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, XUP may process the following categories of information.

### 2.1 Account Information

XUP uses XyberIdentity, XYBERNA's central authentication service.

Information may include:

- Unique account identifier.
- Authentication and session information.
- Basic profile information.
- Account creation and authentication timestamps.
- Account preferences.

XUP does not require users to create a separate authentication identity when signing in through XyberIdentity.

### 2.2 Profile Information

Depending on available features, we may process:

- Display name.
- Profile photograph.
- Username or account identifier.
- User-provided profile information.

### 2.3 Messages and Communications

When you use XUP's communication features, information may include:

- Messages you send and receive.
- Conversation identifiers.
- Message timestamps.
- Delivery and read status.
- Related communication metadata.

Messages are processed to provide the communication services requested by users.

### 2.4 Media and Attachments

When supported features are used, XUP may process:

- Images.
- Videos.
- Documents.
- Other supported attachments.

Media files may be stored on XYBERNA-managed infrastructure to provide access to authorised users.

### 2.5 Technical Information

We may process technical information necessary to operate, maintain and secure XUP, including:

- Application version.
- Device platform.
- Operating system information.
- Connection and request metadata.
- Diagnostic information.
- Security and error logs.

We use this information primarily for troubleshooting, service reliability and security.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information processed by XUP may be used to:

- Authenticate users.
- Maintain account sessions.
- Enable communication between users.
- Deliver messages and supported attachments.
- Synchronise relevant account information.
- Maintain application functionality.
- Detect suspicious activity and prevent abuse.
- Investigate technical problems.
- Improve application reliability.
- Protect the security of our infrastructure.

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to operating, securing or improving our services without an appropriate legal basis.

## 4. XyberIdentity Integration

XUP integrates with XyberIdentity, XYBERNA's central identity and authentication platform.

XyberIdentity provides authentication services across supported XYBERNA applications.

When signing in:

1. You are redirected to the XyberIdentity authentication service.
2. You authenticate using your XyberIdentity account.
3. XyberIdentity provides the necessary authentication response.
4. XUP validates the authentication and establishes an application session.

XUP uses the account identifier and authentication information required to associate your identity with your XUP account.

XyberIdentity and XUP are components of the XYBERNA ecosystem, but authentication information and application data serve different purposes.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

XYBERNA uses managed infrastructure to operate XUP.

Depending on the information involved, data may be stored in:

- Database infrastructure.
- Persistent media storage.
- Application infrastructure.
- Security and diagnostic systems.

We implement reasonable technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information against:

- Unauthorised access.
- Unauthorised modification.
- Accidental loss.
- Unauthorised disclosure.
- Service disruption.

Security measures may include access controls, authentication, protected communications, restricted infrastructure access and secure configuration practices.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

XYBERNA does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be disclosed in limited circumstances:

### 6.1 Other XUP Users

Information you intentionally share, including messages, profile information and attachments, may be visible to their intended recipients.

### 6.2 Infrastructure and Service Providers

Information may be processed by infrastructure or technology providers necessary to operate XUP.

Such providers should only receive information necessary to perform their services.

### 6.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by applicable law, valid legal process or a competent governmental authority.

### 6.4 Security and Abuse Prevention

Information may be processed or disclosed when reasonably necessary to investigate security incidents, prevent abuse or protect users and XYBERNA.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods may depend on:

- Account status.
- Message and communication requirements.
- Security and diagnostic requirements.
- Legal obligations.
- Backup and recovery procedures.

Deleting information from an active system does not necessarily mean that every backup copy is immediately erased.

## 8. Account Deletion and Data Requests

Users may request account deletion or the deletion of personal information associated with their XUP account.

Requests can be submitted through the official XYBERNA support channel.

When processing a deletion request, we will:

1. Verify the request where reasonably necessary.
2. Identify the relevant account information.
3. Delete or anonymise eligible information.
4. Retain information only where required or permitted by applicable law.

Certain information may remain temporarily in backups or records required for legitimate security and legal purposes.

Account deletion in XUP and deletion of a XyberIdentity account may involve separate processes.
