**Last Updated:** 9 July 2026

XyberTask does **not** require you to create an account.

Google AdMob may collect certain information, such as device identifiers and advertising identifiers, to provide and improve advertisements.

For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

All task data is stored locally on your device using local storage.

If you uninstall the application, locally stored data may be removed from your device.
