# Privacy Policy

**XyberFlap by XYBERNA**

**Effective Date:** 23 November 2026  
**Last Updated:** 23 November 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to XyberFlap, developed and published by **XYBERNA**.

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how XyberFlap handles information when you download, install, and use our application.

XyberFlap is designed as an offline-first arcade game. Core gameplay does not require an account, registration, or an internet connection.

By using XyberFlap, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information We Do Not Directly Collect

XYBERNA does not directly require or collect the following information for ordinary gameplay:

- Your real name.
- Your home address.
- Your telephone number.
- Your personal contacts.
- Your photographs or personal files.
- Your precise geographical location.
- Your account passwords.

XyberFlap does not require users to create an account or register to access its core gameplay.

### 2.2 Locally Stored Game Information

XyberFlap may store certain gameplay information directly on your device, including:

- High scores and personal records.
- Game statistics and achievements.
- Unlocked characters and environments.
- Campaign and mission progress.
- Game settings and preferences.
- Selected characters and other gameplay preferences.

This information is used to provide game functionality and maintain your progress.

Locally stored information is not automatically uploaded to XYBERNA servers.

Uninstalling the application or clearing its storage may permanently remove locally stored game information.

## 3. Advertising and Google AdMob

XyberFlap may display advertisements through **Google AdMob**, a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

AdMob may collect or process certain information to deliver, measure, and improve advertisements.

Depending on the applicable advertising configuration and your consent, this information may include:

- Advertising identifiers.
- Device information.
- IP address.
- Advertising interactions.
- App usage information.
- Diagnostic and performance information.

Google may use this information for advertising, fraud prevention, analytics, and other purposes described in its privacy documentation.

### 3.1 Types of Advertisements

XyberFlap may include:

- Banner advertisements.
- Interstitial advertisements.
- Optional rewarded advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements are optional. Users are not required to watch advertisements to access essential gameplay.

Advertisements will not intentionally interrupt active gameplay.

### 3.2 Advertising Personalisation

Depending on your location, applicable laws, and advertising consent settings, advertisements may be personalised or non-personalised.

Where required, the application will provide an appropriate consent mechanism before using advertising technologies that require consent.

You can manage certain advertising preferences through your Google account and device settings.

For more information, visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## 6. Internet Connectivity

XyberFlap is designed to support offline gameplay.

An internet connection may be required for:

- Loading advertisements.
- Processing optional purchases.
- Updating the application.
- Accessing third-party services.

If an internet connection is unavailable, advertising and other online-dependent services may not function.

Core gameplay should remain accessible without an internet connection.

## 7. Data Sharing

XYBERNA does not sell users' personal information.

We do not directly share locally stored gameplay information with advertisers or other companies.

However, third-party services integrated into the application, particularly Google AdMob, may independently collect and process information as described in their respective privacy policies.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or valid legal processes.

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application.

Locally stored gameplay information remains on your device unless you choose to share it through another service.

However, no electronic storage system or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Children's Privacy

XyberFlap is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

We do not require account registration or personal information to play the game.

Advertising content and advertising practices will be configured in accordance with applicable platform policies and privacy laws.

Where required, appropriate safeguards and consent mechanisms will be implemented.

If you believe that personal information has been inadvertently collected from a child in a manner that violates applicable law, please contact us.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy legislation, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including:

- Requesting information about personal data processing.
- Requesting access to personal information.
- Requesting correction or deletion of applicable personal information.
- Withdrawing consent where processing relies on consent.
- Managing advertising preferences.

For information processed independently by Google, you may need to exercise your rights directly through Google's privacy tools.

## 11. Data Retention and Deletion

XyberFlap's locally stored gameplay information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the application's storage, or uninstall the application.

Information independently collected by third-party services is retained according to their respective retention policies.

XYBERNA does not maintain a central account database for ordinary XyberFlap gameplay.
