## 1. Introduction

Welcome to XyberBreak, an arcade game developed and published by XYBERNA.

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use XyberBreak (the "App").

By using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the practices described here, please discontinue using the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

XyberBreak is designed primarily as an Android gaming experience. The information processed depends on the features you use and the third-party services integrated into the App.

### 2.1 Gameplay Data

The App may store gameplay information locally on your device, including:

- Game progress and unlocked levels.
- High scores and personal records.
- Game settings and preferences.
- Achievement and progression information.
- Other information necessary for the game to function.

This information is used to provide and improve your gaming experience.

Unless a feature explicitly states otherwise, locally stored gameplay data is not automatically uploaded to XYBERNA servers.

### 2.2 Advertising Data

The App may use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

- Advertising identifiers.
- Device information and identifiers.
- IP address.
- Advertising interactions and impressions.
- App activity and diagnostic information.
- Approximate location inferred from IP address, where applicable.

The information collected depends on your device, advertising settings, consent choices, applicable law, and Google's advertising technologies.

Advertising data may be used to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and support advertising-related services.

For further information, please review Google's privacy documentation:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 2.3 Diagnostic and Technical Information

Depending on the SDKs and services included in the App, technical information may be processed to identify errors, diagnose problems, maintain stability, and improve performance.

Such information may include device specifications, operating system version, crash reports, and application diagnostics.

The information actually collected depends on the services enabled in the published version.
