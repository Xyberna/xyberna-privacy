**Privacy Policy – XyberLight**

XyberLight is developed by XYBERNA.

XyberLight is a simple flashlight utility application. The app itself does not collect, store, or share any personal user data.

Permissions used by the app:

Camera Permission
The app uses the device camera flash to provide flashlight functionality. The camera is never used to capture photos or videos.

Internet Permission
The app uses internet access to load advertisements.

Advertising
This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device information such as device identifiers, IP address, and usage data in order to serve relevant advertisements. This data is handled according to Google's privacy policies.
